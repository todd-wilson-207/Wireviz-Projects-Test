# LACE Controller Power Harness

## 1. Scope

This harness connects the LACE controller board to the interface board. Typical potential is 12 V

## 2. Tools & Materials

| Item           | Specification            |
|----------------|--------------------------|
| Crimp tool     | Molex 63811 series       |
| Heat-shrink    | 2:1 small                |

## 3. Assembly Steps

1. Cut cables **W1** and **W2** to length and strip 1.5 mm on each conductor.
2. Crimp contacts onto each conductor and seat them in the housings.
3. Apply heat-shrink over the housing entries for strain relief.

## 4. Test

- Continuity-check every pin pair against the diagram and pinouts in the related schematic files.
- Functional check per the applicable procedure.
