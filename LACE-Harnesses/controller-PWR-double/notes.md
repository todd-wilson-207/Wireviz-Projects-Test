## 2. Tools & Materials

| Item           | Specification            |
|----------------|--------------------------|
| Crimp tool     | Molex 63811 series       |
| Heat-shrink    | 3:1 adhesive-lined, 3 mm |

## 3. Assembly Steps

1. Cut cable **W1** to length and strip 4 mm on each conductor.
2. Crimp contacts onto each conductor and seat them in the housings.
3. Apply heat-shrink over the housing entries for strain relief.

## 4. Test

- Continuity-check every pin pair against the bill of materials (sheet 3).
- Functional check per the applicable procedure.
