\hypertarget{1-scope}{%
\subsection{1. Scope}\label{1-scope}}

Assembly of harness \textbf{HRN-0042}, connecting a DB9 (X1) RS-232 port to a USB-A (X2) host lead through an FT232RL module (U1). Refer to the wiring diagram on sheet 2 and the bill of materials on
sheet 3.

\hypertarget{3-assembly-steps}{%
\subsection{3. Assembly Steps}\label{3-assembly-steps}}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  Cut cable \textbf{W1} to 300 mm and \textbf{W2} to 150 mm; strip 4 mm on each conductor.
\item
  Tin every U1 module pad before attaching wires.
\item
  Solder \textbf{W1} conductors to U1 per the colour map on sheet 2.
\item
  Solder \textbf{W2} RD / BK to U1 VCC / GND.
\item
  Crimp DB9 (X1) contacts onto \textbf{W1}; seat each contact until the lock tab latches.
\item
  Terminate USB-A (X2): VBUS to RD, GND to BK. Leave D- / D+ unconnected.
\item
  Apply heat-shrink over every solder joint and over the U1 module body.
\item
  Strain-relieve both cable entries with a zip tie 10 mm from the housing.
\end{enumerate}

\hypertarget{4-test}{%
\subsection{4. Test}\label{4-test}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Continuity-check every pin pair against the bill of materials (sheet 3).
\item
  Insulation resistance, X1 shell to all pins: \textbf{\textgreater{} 20 Mohm at 250 V}.
\item
  Functional: enumerate on the host, loop TXD-RXD at X1, confirm echo at 115200 baud.
\end{itemize}
